The data

Five excerpts about gatherings held in 10 Downing Street in 2020 and 2021, while covid rules were in force. On 30 November 2021 the Daily Mirror reported that gatherings had been held there in November and December 2020 in breach of the rules. In Excerpts A to C the Prime Minister, Boris Johnson, speaks at Prime Minister’s Questions, and in Excerpt D an opposition leader replies to a statement of his. The police later issued fixed-penalty notices for several of the gatherings, one of them to Johnson. In June 2023 the Committee reported that he had misled the House, a finding that Johnson rejected.

The rows already filled in are models to follow, and short notes are enough in the cells.

Grice’s maxims
Quantity 1Make your contribution as informative as is required.
Quantity 2Do not make your contribution more informative than is required.
RelationBe relevant.
MannerBe perspicuous. 1 Avoid obscurity of expression. 2 Avoid ambiguity. 3 Be brief. 4 Be orderly.
Horn’s principles
QMake your contribution sufficient. Say as much as you can, given R. It collects Quantity 1 and Manner 1 and 2.
RMake your contribution necessary. Say no more than you must, given Q. It collects Quantity 2, Relation and Manner 3 and 4.
Q, scalarOn a scale such as ⟨hot, warm⟩, saying the weaker item implicates that the stronger one does not hold.
Q, division of labourWhere a marked and an unmarked expression could say the same thing, the unmarked one is used for the stereotypical situation, by R. The marked one implicates, by Q, that the situation is not stereotypical.
Levinson’s heuristics
QWhat isn’t said, isn’t. From Quantity 1.
Q, scalarAs for Horn.
Q, clausalWhere a stronger form would entail an embedded clause p and the form used does not, the speaker implicates that she does not know whether p. I believe she’s in, beside I know she’s in.
IWhat is expressed simply is stereotypically exemplified. From Quantity 2. Its kinds include conditional perfection, reading if as only if, and conjunction buttressing, reading and as and then.
MWhat’s said in an abnormal way isn’t normal. From Manner 1 and 3. A marked expression conveys that the situation is not stereotypical.
noneThe heuristics cover generalised implicatures only. A particularised implicature, which depends on the particular context, has no heuristic.

Cancellation

Definitions
GeneralisedAn implicature an expression carries in most contexts, unless something blocks it. Levinson treats these as default inferences. To test for one, ask whether the same words would carry it after a different question.
ParticularisedAn implicature that depends on the particular context of the utterance, such as the question just asked. Change the question and the inference goes.
CancelledSomething inconsistent with the implicature is in the context or is added to it, so the implicature is not added. No contradiction results. It can happen in the same sentence, as in Some or all of the students passed, or later.
ReinforcedThe speaker makes the implicature explicit, without redundancy, as in Some of the students passed, but not all.
SurvivesNothing in the data cancels it.
