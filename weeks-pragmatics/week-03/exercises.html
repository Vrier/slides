Implicature Data Analysis

Data. Excerpts from the official transcript of an exchange between President Donald Trump and reporters in the lobby of Trump Tower, New York, on 15 August 2017. It followed remarks on infrastructure, and came three days after the Unite the Right rally in Charlottesville, Virginia, at which a counter-protester, Heather Heyer, was killed. Source: Daily Compilation of Presidential Documents, DCPD-201700573.

Questions

  1. In (18) and (22) reporters put into words what they took Trump to implicate. What did they take him to mean, and how does each of his answers respond?
  2. In (20) Trump says he is not talking about the neo-Nazis and white nationalists. What is he cancelling, and does the cancellation work by the lecture’s test? Where does he reinforce an implicature instead?
  3. What stronger alternatives were available in (14) and (15)? Does competence hold for Trump here, and what follows if it does?
  4. What do even, also and but contribute in (6), (13) and (19)? Rephrase each line without them: what is lost, and what does that show?
