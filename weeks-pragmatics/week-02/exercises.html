Speech Acts Data Analysis

Data. Excerpts from the official transcript of a meeting in the Oval Office on 28 February 2025 between President Donald Trump, Vice President JD Vance and President Volodymyr Zelenskyy of Ukraine, three years into Russia’s invasion of Ukraine. The meeting, held in front of the press, was to precede the signing of an agreement on Ukraine’s minerals; after this exchange the signing and a joint press conference were cancelled, and Zelenskyy left the White House. Source: Daily Compilation of Presidential Documents, DCPD-202500310.

Questions

  1. Vance and Trump demand thanks from Zelenskyy, who says he has already given it. Can thanks be demanded, and what is a demanded thank-you worth?
  2. In (11) Zelenskyy says the United States will feel the war in the future, and Trump hears him dictating. What did Zelenskyy do, and who decides?
  3. Who has the power to do things with words in this room? Look at who asks for the floor and who grants or refuses it in (3), (4), (19) and (20), and at what (28) and (29) do.
  4. Does it matter to the speech act in (22) whether the figure of $350 billion is true, and to what extent?
  5. Trump says this is good for the American people to see (24) and will make great television (30). Who is the meeting for, and how does that change what its acts are?
