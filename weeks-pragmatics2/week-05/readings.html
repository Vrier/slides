Week 5 · Readings

Probability and Priors

Reading list Figures: Pascal · Bayes · Ramsey · de Finetti · Degen

Four readings for the week: an interactive textbook, a contemporary paper, and two free online introductions that track the lecture closely and assume little mathematics. Confirm against Blackboard before circulating.

Reading list textbook · contemporary · two online
  1. TextbookKunin, D., Guo, J., Devlin, T. D., & Xiang, D. (n.d.). Seeing theory: A visual introduction to probability and statistics. Brown University.
    An interactive online textbook. Chapters 1, 2, 3 and 5 (probability, conditional probability, distributions, Bayesian inference) match this week.
    Open accessOpen
  2. ContemporaryTonhauser, J., Beaver, D. I., & Degen, J. (2018). How projective is projective content? Gradience in projectivity and at-issueness. Journal of Semantics, 35(3), 495–542. https://doi.org/10.1093/jos/ffy007
    Projection comes in degrees: the background to the slide on prior beliefs and projection.
    Library loginDOI
  3. OnlineOlah, C. (2015, October 14). Visual information theory. colah’s blog.
    From probability distributions to entropy, with pictures in place of formulas.
    Open accessOpen
  4. OnlineArbital. (n.d.). Bayes’ rule. Hosted on LessWrong.
    Bayes’ rule in odds form with Bayes factors, with worked examples at several levels.
    Open accessOpen
Further online reading free · more mathematical
  1. FurtherNicenboim, B., Schad, D. J., & Vasishth, S. (n.d.). An introduction to Bayesian data analysis for cognitive science (chaps. 1 and 13).
    Probability and distributions (chapter 1) and Bayes factors (chapter 13), written for psycholinguists.
    Open accessOpen
  2. FurtherMacKay, D. J. C. (2003). Probability, entropy, and inference. In Information theory, inference, and learning algorithms (chap. 2). Cambridge University Press.
    Probability and entropy in one chapter; the most mathematical item here.
    Open accessOpen PDF
