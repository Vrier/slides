Problem set for Week 1. Week 1 sits in no single corner, so these problems set up the two diagnostics you will carry through the whole term and ask you to state, in advance, what each approach owes you. Short answers throughout.

1 Scalar implicature — marks

Take Some of the students passed, heard as not all of them did.

(a) State the inference precisely. (b) Write a continuation that cancels it without contradiction. (c) In two sentences, say what the cancellability shows about where the inference comes from.

2 Presupposition — marks

Take Alex stopped smoking.

(a) Give the negated version and state what each version asserts. (b) Identify the content common to both. (c) Try the cancellation move from problem 1 on that content and record what goes wrong.

3 Three corners, in advance — marks

Each approach has to derive the scalar inference in problem 1. Sketch, in two or three sentences each, how Inference, Grammar and Information would go about it. Accuracy matters less here than making the three derivations genuinely different from one another.

4 The course question — marks

Does mastering statistical structure deliver grammar and inference for free? Name one finding that would push you towards yes and one that would push you towards no. Keep both concrete enough that someone could go and look for them.
