Q6. Pierre’s puzzle: an essay(20 points)

Pierre grows up in France speaking only French. From postcards, he comes to say sincerely, Londres est jolie ‘London is pretty’. Later he moves to a run-down part of London, learns English without translating, and says sincerely, London is not pretty. He never finds out that the two names name one city.

  1. (27)Pierre believes that London is pretty.
  2. (28)Pierre believes that London is not pretty.

Assume that London and Londres are rigid names of the same city.

Is Kripke’s puzzle a problem of hyperintensionality? Write one contiguous essay which addresses all of the following points. (Approx 1000 words)

  1. (i)Explain why (27) and (28) both seem true, and why the puzzle arises without substituting one expression for another.
  2. (ii)Show what (27) and (28) together say about DoxPierre(w), given rigid names and the Hintikka entry for believe.
  3. (iii)Is the position of London in (27) hyperintensional? Is hyperintensionality what goes wrong for Pierre?
  4. (iv)Suggest a possible repair to the semantics of belief that would solve the puzzle. Explain how it handles (27) and (28), and what it gets wrong.
  5. (v)Reach a conclusion, and answer the strongest objection to it.
