Week 9 · Readings

Conditionals

Reading list Figures: Kratzer · Lewis · Stalnaker

Six sources for Week 9: the textbook chapter on conditionals and its section on non-monotonicity, three classical sources (the selection function, the system of spheres, the restrictor thesis) and two contemporary pieces (a handbook survey of the restrictor analysis and the yo-yo puzzle). References are in APA; open-access links are given where available. Read the textbook chapter first, then Kratzer. Confirm editions and availability against the library / Blackboard list before circulating.

Reading list one textbook · three classical · two contemporary
  1. Textbookvon Fintel, K., & Heim, I. (2011). Conditionals; Non-monotonicity of conditionals. In Intensional semantics (ch. 4 and §5.4) [Lecture notes]. Massachusetts Institute of Technology.
    The material and strict analyses and their failures on the Lockhart example, then if-clauses as restrictors of modals; §5.4 adds Sobel sequences and the three invalid inferences.
    Open access Open PDF
  2. ClassicalStalnaker, R. C. (1968). A theory of conditionals. In N. Rescher (Ed.), Studies in logical theory (pp. 98–112). Blackwell.
    The selection function and the Ramsey test as a semantics: one closest antecedent world, hence conditional excluded middle.
    Library login DOI
  3. ClassicalLewis, D. (1973). Counterfactuals (§§1.1–1.6). Blackwell.
    Spheres of similarity and the variably strict conditional; kangaroos, the Sobel sequence, and the case against uniqueness.
    Purchase
  4. ClassicalKratzer, A. (1986). Conditionals. Chicago Linguistic Society, 22(2), 1–15. Revised as ch. 4 of Modals and conditionals (2012, Oxford University Press).
    The restrictor thesis: there is no two-place if … then connective; the if-clause restricts a modal or an adverb of quantification.
    Open access Author copy
  5. Contemporaryvon Fintel, K. (2011). Conditionals. In K. von Heusinger, C. Maienborn, & P. Portner (Eds.), Semantics: An international handbook of natural language meaning (Vol. 2, pp. 1515–1538). De Gruyter.
    A survey of the restrictor analysis and its competitors, with the arguments from modals, adverbs and probability laid out in order.
    Open access Open PDF
  6. Contemporaryvon Fintel, K., & Gillies, A. S. (2026). Yo-yo conditionals and counterfactual suppositions [Handout]. University of Texas at Austin. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22116790
    Iterated counterfactuals whose inner antecedent takes back the outer one, which every standard semantics predicts to be fine, and three ways of repairing the prediction.
    Open access Zenodo
