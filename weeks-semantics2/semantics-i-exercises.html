Sets, relations and functions

(a)Give the cardinality of each set.
i. {∅}   ii. {a, a, b}   iii. {{a, b}, a, b}   iv. {13, {7, 66, 1}}
(b)Decide: true or false.
i. {3, 3, 2} = {3, 2}   ii. {∅} = ∅   iii. ∅ ⊆ {∅}   iv. {a} ∈ {a, b}   v. {a} ⊆ {a, b}   vi. ⟨a, b⟩ = ⟨b, a⟩
(c)Let A = {1, 2, 3, 4} and B = {3, 4, 5}. Compute A ∩ B, A ∪ B, A − B, B − A and |A × B|.

  • ii.Rory is tall but he isn’t sleepy.
  • iii.No linguist is a philosopher.
  • iv.All happy dogs bark.
(e)Decide which relations are functions, with a reason for each.
i. people to their birthdays   ii. people to their siblings   iii. {⟨1, a⟩, ⟨2, a⟩}   iv. {⟨1, a⟩, ⟨1, b⟩}

Predicate logic

Model M2. D2 = {Anna, Brian, Ciara, Rex}. I2(a) = Anna, I2(b) = Brian, I2(c) = Ciara, I2(r) = Rex. I2(linguist) = {Anna, Brian}. I2(dog) = {Rex}. I2(happy) = {Anna, Rex}. I2(admires) = {⟨Anna, Brian⟩, ⟨Brian, Anna⟩, ⟨Ciara, Anna⟩, ⟨Rex, Anna⟩}.

(a)Translate into predicate logic.
  • i.Anna admires Brian and Brian isn’t happy.
  • ii.If Ciara is a linguist, then Ciara admires herself.
  • iii.Either Anna admires Brian or Brian admires Anna, but not both.
(b)Compute the truth value of each translation in M2. Show every membership check.
(c)Change I2 minimally so that (ii) comes out false.

Coppock & Champollion. Invitation to Formal Semantics. §4.1.

(b)Evaluate each translation in M2 (block 3). For each, name the individual or individuals k whose assignment g[x ↦ k] settles the value.
(c)Some student read every book. Give a formula for each reading and a paraphrase of each. State which reading entails the other.
(d)Mark each variable occurrence as bound or free.

Coppock & Champollion. Invitation to Formal Semantics. §4.2.

Generalised quantifiers

(a)Give DET(A)(B) in set-theoretic terms for at least three, exactly one, fewer than three and more than half.
(b)Write ⟦no dog⟧ and ⟦some dog⟧ as sets of sets. Then decide which of I2(linguist), I2(happy) and D2 are members of ⟦every linguist⟧ in M2 (block 3).
(c)Three fictional determiners:
  • i.only(A)(B) = B ⊆ A
  • ii.odd(A)(B) = |A ∩ B| is odd

For each, state whether it is conservative, and whether it is upward monotone, downward monotone or non-monotone in B. Justify each answer with sets or with a test sentence pair.
(d)State both readings of Two students passed in set-theoretic terms. Give the monotonicity of each.
