Week 6 · Readings

Hyperintensionality

Reading list Figures: Brouwer · Carnap · Cresswell · Barwise & Perry · Fine

Four sources for Week 6: a textbook survey of hyperintensionality and three contemporary papers, one on structured propositions and two on truthmaker semantics. Read the survey’s introduction first. References are in APA; open-access links are given where available. Confirm editions and availability against the library / Blackboard list before circulating.

Reading list one textbook · three contemporary

    Where hyperintensional contexts show up, and the main ways of modelling them.

    Truthmaker semantics applied to attitude verbs, modals and intensional transitive verbs.
